Behind on your mortgage? You have options.

We're local Colorado real estate investors. If payments have gotten hard, we'll talk through your situation with you, with no pressure and no obligation.

Your options usually include

Work it out with your lenderA repayment plan, loan modification or forbearance may let you stay.
Refinance or catch upPaying what's past due before the sale can stop the foreclosure.
Sell with an agentListing on the open market can bring the highest price if there's time.
Sell directly to usWe may be able to buy as-is, and in some cases take over your existing loan payments and catch up what's behind. Every term is in writing, and you can review it with anyone you trust before signing.

Not every option fits every home. If we take over payments on your existing loan, the loan stays in your name until it's paid off or refinanced, so we'll explain how that works and what protects you before anything is signed.
